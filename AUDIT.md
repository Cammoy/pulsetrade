# PulseTrade — Production-Readiness & Profit Audit

_Generated 2026-06-15 · multi-agent audit, 53/55 findings verified · verdict: **NOT-READY**_

## Executive summary

PulseTrade is a competently-structured paper-trading engine with a genuinely dangerous live-money path. The single most important truth: in LIVE mode the entire reported ledger — position qty, avgCost, realized/unrealized P&L, win/loss, equity curve, the principal-vs-profit sweep, and the daily-spend cap — is computed from a CoinGecko/pool QUOTE price, never from the swap that actually executed (settle commits execBuy/execSell on the spot `price`, ignoring the measured on-chain delta `r.delta` that mirrorLive already returns; engine.ts:406-411, 315/343). So the dashboard can show a winner while the wallet is losing real money, and on the thin pools the sniper targets the gap is largest. On top of that the live account is exposed by configuration, not just code: server/.env has NO ENGINE_TOKEN (auth fully off — `if (!ENGINE_TOKEN) return next()`, index.ts:76) while Turnkey Solana signing IS armed, so any local/LAN caller can flip live mode and drain the wallet via Jupiter; and there is NO ZEROX_API_KEY, so every 'live' EVM bot silently paper-fills phantom trades while consuming the daily cap. There is no global kill-switch or cumulative-loss circuit breaker (only a per-day BUY cap, default 0=off in code, set to 50 in this .env), the daily cap is non-atomic under concurrent ticks, the manual-stop liquidation books paper P&L BEFORE a fire-and-forget on-chain sell (corrupting the ledger forever on failure), no money-path fetch has a timeout, and prices never expire. The backtest/Profit Finder cost model (0.15%/side) is 3-10x below real live cost and ranks the single most overfit config — so the numbers the owner would use to pick what to trade are systematically optimistic. None of this is unfixable, but the bot must NOT trade real money in its current state.

## Profit verdict

Honest answer: as written, this is much more likely to slowly (or quickly) lose the few-hundred-dollar account than to grow it, and the goal of 10x/month to quit a day job is not achievable — not by this bot, and not by any retail crypto bot. Here is why, grounded in the code. (1) The reported edge is fictional in live mode: P&L is a CoinGecko-spot simulation, not wallet-actual (engine.ts:406-411), so you literally cannot tell from the dashboard whether you are up or down. (2) The edge that the backtest shows is over-credited: the model charges 0.15%/side + ~$0.001-0.10 gas (backtest.ts:24,87,99), while live tolerates 1% slippage on 0x/Jupiter (live.ts:79, live-solana.ts:94) plus LP fee (~0.30%), price impact on thin pools, a second approve-gas leg on EVM sells, and MEV/sandwich extraction in the public mempool — realistically 1.5-3+ percentage points of cost PER round trip that the backtest never charges. At a $36-$66 position size (risk.ts positionPct 12/22), fixed gas + the approve tx is a proportionally huge drag. (3) Profit Finder reports the best-of-15 in-sample fit on a single 30d window, and top100/trending backtest only coins that ALREADY pumped (backtest.ts:276-279, 355-360) — pure survivorship/selection bias, so its headline number systematically overstates forward returns. (4) The default 'All-Weather' bot is marketed as 'Profits in any market' (web/src/App.tsx:113) but its crash defense is price 6% below a 50-HOUR SMA — structurally too slow for a fast dump; it is a capital-preserving default, not a money-maker. (5) The sniper, the highest-upside path, is also the highest-probability total-loss path: 100% of bot budget into a minutes-old token (strategies.ts:204) behind a point-in-time, single-provider-defeatable safety screen, with no price-impact guard. The realistic expectation is small, noisy returns at best after honest costs, with a real chance of ruin on a single bad sniper token or a silent live/paper desync. If you want this to ever be net-positive live, the levers below are necessary but still not sufficient to beat fees+MEV reliably on a small account.

## P0 — must fix before ANY real money (loses funds / leaks wallet / corrupts ledger)

### P0-1. Live P&L, position, and sweep are derived from the quote price, never reconciled to the measured on-chain fill
- **Where:** `server/src/engine.ts:406-411, 315/343, 94-118, 121-181`
- **Impact:** In live mode every number the owner uses to judge the bot is fiction. settle() commits execBuy/execSell on the CoinGecko/pool quote `price` and ignores r.delta — the true on-chain fill mirrorLive already measured (engine.ts:315/343, returned at 317). position.qty, avgCostUsd, realized/unrealized P&L, win/loss, the equity curve, and the principal-vs-profit native sweep are all wrong; on thin sniper pools the real fill is materially below the paper qty, so the dashboard can show a winner while the wallet loses. realHeld and position.qty silently diverge; reconcileRealHeld only repairs realHeld, never the position/P&L.
- **Fix:** Thread LiveResult.delta into the commit: realQty = Number(r.delta.baseUnits)/10**r.delta.decimals; pass it to execBuy so position.qty += realQty and avgCostUsd derives from actual native spent over realQty. Also capture native before/after to get realized USD. Do the symmetric thing on sells (measured sold delta = realized basis). After every confirmed fill, reconcile position.qty to realHeld so TP/SL logic runs on units actually owned. Keep the spot path only for paper mode.

### P0-2. Live Solana trading is armed with auth fully disabled — wallet drainable by any local/LAN caller
- **Where:** `server/src/index.ts:75-82, 293-306, 398-403; server/.env (no ENGINE_TOKEN)`
- **Impact:** Confirmed: server/.env sets all TURNKEY_* keys + SOLANA_ADDRESS but NO ENGINE_TOKEN, so solanaLiveReady() is true and the auth middleware short-circuits open (`if (!ENGINE_TOKEN) return next()`). server.listen() passes no host, binding 0.0.0.0 (LAN-reachable). Any local process/user or LAN host can POST /api/mode {mode:'live'} and create bots/snipers that execute real Jupiter swaps signed by the Turnkey wallet, with no credential. Only the $50/day BUY cap brakes it, and that bounds neither sells nor sweeps and resets daily.
- **Fix:** Fail-closed at startup: if ((liveReady()||solanaLiveReady()) && !ENGINE_TOKEN) console.error+process.exit(1) instead of only warning. Bind to loopback by default: server.listen(PORT, process.env.ENGINE_HOST || '127.0.0.1'). Set a strong ENGINE_TOKEN in server/.env and matching VITE_ENGINE_TOKEN before any live use. Add a Turnkey policy whitelisting destinations/limits as defense in depth.

### P0-3. Manual/auto stop books paper liquidation BEFORE the fire-and-forget on-chain sell — failure corrupts the money ledger permanently
- **Where:** `server/src/engine.ts:1188-1201 (and 881, 997, 1103, 1157)`
- **Impact:** controlBot stop calls execSell(...sweepAll=true) synchronously, then fires `void mirrorLive(...).then(flushNow).catch(()=>{})` and sets status='stopped' unconditionally. This inverts settle()'s mirror-first contract. If the real sell fails (no liquidity, RPC down, revert, honeypot that blocks selling — exactly the sniper exposure), the ledger records the position sold-and-swept with phantom realized P&L and inflated cash, while real tokens stay in the wallet. No retry (stopped bots early-return), and reconcileRealHeld repairs only realHeld, never cash/position/stats/monthly. Fans out to tournament end, auto-sniper stop, and follow stop.
- **Fix:** Make stop symmetric with settle: await mirrorLive('sell',...) FIRST; only on r.ok run execSell(...sweepAll) + set status='stopped' + flush. On !r.ok do NOT mutate paper state — pause and log so the operator can retry. This makes controlBot async; update the API handler, resolveTournaments, stopAutoSniper, stopFollow to await it. Stop swallowing the error in .catch. Add a regression test asserting a rejecting mirrorLive leaves position/cash/stats/monthly unchanged and status non-stopped.

### P0-4. EVM live is silently disarmed — missing ZEROX_API_KEY makes 'live' EVM bots paper-commit phantom fills and burn the daily cap
- **Where:** `server/src/engine.ts:260-263, 406-418; server/src/live.ts:16; server/.env (no ZEROX_API_KEY)`
- **Impact:** Confirmed: no ZEROX_API_KEY in .env, so liveReady() is false for all EVM chains, yet the system arms live (Solana satisfies the OR-guard at index.ts:296). mirrorLive returns {ok:true} for unconfigured EVM (engine.ts:260-263), so settle falls through to execBuy/execSell committing a paper fill that never touched chain, AND runs recordDailySpend for money that never moved — so the $50/day cap is consumed by phantom buys. The owner believes base/arbitrum/optimism/polygon/bnb bots trade real funds; they do not. Only signal is one buried per-bot warn.
- **Fix:** Make mirrorLive return {ok:false} (routes through haltLive, pauses bot) instead of {ok:true} when a bot is on an EVM network in live mode but liveReady() is false. Add a boot-time env-coherence check: if turnkeyEvmReady() is true but ZEROX_API_KEY is unset, fail loud at startup. Ops: add the real ZEROX_API_KEY and confirm /api/meta shows evm.ready===true before any live EVM tick.

### P0-5. No global kill-switch or cumulative-loss circuit breaker — only a per-day BUY cap brakes a live account
- **Where:** `server/src/engine.ts:365-383, 394-421, 423-435; server/src/store.ts:198-206`
- **Impact:** A losing live session can bleed the ~$300 account toward zero with no automatic stop. The only account-level gate in the money path is checkDailyCap, which bounds same-day BUY notional only and resets on day-roll; sells/sweeps are uncapped. State carries no lifetime-spend, cumulative-loss, or high-water/drawdown field, and nothing reads bot.stats.realizedUsd to force-pause on losses. Code default of MAX_LIVE_USD_PER_DAY is 0 = unlimited (this .env sets 50, which still bounds only daily buy flow).
- **Fix:** Add an account-level circuit breaker checked in settle() before any live buy: track cumulative realized P&L and live equity vs a persisted high-water mark, force ALL bots to 'paused' when MAX_CUMULATIVE_LOSS_USD or MAX_DRAWDOWN_PCT is breached, requiring explicit manual re-arm (no auto day-roll reset). Add a persisted MAX_LIVE_USD_LIFETIME cap and a per-trade max-USD bound. Make the code default of MAX_LIVE_USD_PER_DAY a small non-zero value so an unconfigured live run is bounded.

### P0-6. Sniper commits 100% of bot budget into minutes-old tokens behind a point-in-time, single-provider-defeatable screen, with no price-impact guard
- **Where:** `server/src/strategies.ts:204; server/src/engine.ts:761/823; server/src/sniper.ts:127/153; server/src/risk-screen.ts:90,114-121,162-175`
- **Impact:** For a few-hundred-dollar live account this is the highest-probability total-loss path. Each sniper bot puts its entire allocation into one fresh token (strategies.ts:204 usd: ctx.cashUsd); auto-sniper recycles cash into the next, compounding losses. The screen only blocks provably-bad-now tokens: is_proxy and can_take_back_ownership are merely 'warn' (still tradeable), LP-lock is a one-time read with no post-entry re-check, and a GoPlus-blind contract can clear on honeypot.is alone (sniper.ts:153). No price-impact/pool-reserve check exists anywhere, so a $50-200 buy into a $2k pool is sandwich bait.
- **Fix:** Size each snipe as a small FIXED fraction of total account equity, not 100% of the per-bot budget (change strategies.ts:204). Add an account-equity concurrent-exposure cap independent of the daily cap. Promote is_proxy and can_take_back_ownership to non-tradeable for sniping. Require BOTH safety providers to affirmatively pass for a fresh snipe. Add a pre-entry price-impact check against pool reserves and abort above a threshold.

### P0-7. Stale CoinGecko/pool price never expires — frozen price drives SL/TP and live swap sizing during 429 backoff or outage
- **Where:** `server/src/prices.ts:5,15-22,30-44,65-68; server/src/engine.ts:84-92,280-291,326,437-438`
- **Impact:** Confirmed: spotCache/poolCache are plain Maps with no per-entry timestamp; cgFetch returns null during a 90s backoff (the public tier 429s constantly) and getSpotPrices/getCandles silently serve the old cache. priceForBot/tickBot only reject falsy prices, so a frozen-but-nonzero price drives stop-loss/take-profit (a stop never sees the real drop, position bleeds uncapped) AND sizes live buys (usd/nativePrice on EVM, lamportsForUsd on Solana) — mis-buying real ETH/SOL by the full staleness ratio, invisible to the operator.
- **Fix:** Store fetchedAt per cache entry and enforce a hard staleness ceiling (e.g. 120s spot, 60s pool). In priceForBot/tickBot treat an over-age price as undefined and skip the tick with a visible 'pricing stale — trading halted' warning. Critically, extend the !nativePrice guard in mirrorLive to also throw when the native price is stale, so no live buy is ever sized from a frozen price.

### P0-8. No timeout on any money-path fetch or receipt wait — a single hung connection freezes the lane and stalls the engine
- **Where:** `server/src/live.ts:81,98,108; server/src/live-solana.ts:96,101,120-122; server/src/prices.ts:17,73; server/src/engine.ts:51-62,287/322,575-628`
- **Impact:** An unattended live bot can silently stop trading mid-position with no crash. The only timeout in the server is screen-fetch.ts; every money-path external call runs bare. A black-holed 0x/Jupiter/CoinGecko fetch hangs forever inside runExclusive, wedging that chain's serialized lane so every subsequent buy/sell/stop queues and never fires. A hung price fetch in tick() stalls all bots for the loop. Stop-losses queue behind the hang and never fire; open positions ride uncapped drawdown. (Note: viem's http() does bound waitForTransactionReceipt to ~180s; the genuine unbounded hang is the raw 0x/Jupiter quote fetches.)
- **Fix:** Wrap every live-path fetch in AbortSignal.timeout (reuse the getJson pattern): 0x quote (live.ts:81), both Jupiter fetches (live-solana.ts:96,101), price reads (prices.ts:17,73). Pass an explicit shorter timeout to both waitForTransactionReceipt calls. Bound Solana confirmTransaction with lastValidBlockHeight + a wall-clock cap. A timeout throws → mirrorLive returns {ok:false} → bot pauses cleanly instead of silently freezing.

### P0-9. Backtest/Profit Finder cost model (0.15%/side) is 3-10x below real live cost, and ranks the single most overfit/survivorship-biased config
- **Where:** `server/src/backtest.ts:24,87,99,211-233,276-279,355-360; server/src/live.ts:79; server/src/live-solana.ts:94`
- **Impact:** Every backtest returnPct and Profit Finder profitUsd is over-credited by ~1.5-3+ pp of cost per round trip (more for grid/meanrev doing 20-30+ trips/30d). Live tolerates 1% slippage on 0x/Jupiter plus LP fee, price impact, MEV, and a second approve-gas leg on EVM sells, none of which the model charges. Profit Finder also reports best-of-15 in-sample fit on one 30d window, and top100/trending backtest only already-pumped coins — selection + survivorship bias. Docs promise 'what you backtest is exactly what runs', so the owner has no reason to distrust numbers that systematically overstate forward returns and point most confidently at the thinnest pools.
- **Fix:** Raise the paper/backtest cost model to reality: explicit LP-fee term (~0.30%) PLUS a slippage/impact floor of at least the 1% the live quote tolerates (ideally liquidity-scaled), ~1.0-1.5%/side. Charge a second approve-gas leg on EVM sells. Add walk-forward (fit on earlier window, rank on disjoint later window) and penalize the 15-trial selection (report median, not max). Label top100/trending as survivorship-biased. Correct the README/DOCS backtest==live claims. Best: feed real on-chain realized amounts back into P&L.

## P1 — reliability/correctness that bites in production

1. **Daily live-spend cap is a non-atomic check-then-act across an await — concurrent bots overshoot it by up to K×** — `server/src/engine.ts:401,406,408,607,287/322`  
   _Fix:_ checkDailyCap reads state.liveSpend synchronously, awaits mirrorLive, and only records spend after the swap resolves — all OUTSIDE any runExclusive lane, while all bots tick concurrently via Promise.all. K bots in one tick read the same pre-spend snapshot and all pass. Reserve the spend synchronously BEFORE the await (state.liveSpend = recordDailySpend(...usd...) right after a passing check), refund on !r.ok, and key reserve+refund to the same captured `today`; or wrap check+swap+record in a single 'live-buys' lane. Add a concurrent-buys regression test asserting total committed ≤ cap.
2. **Cap and live P&L record paper-intended USD, not realized native outflow** — `server/src/engine.ts:291,326,397,408; server/src/live-solana.ts:51-54`  
   _Fix:_ The swap is sized from a cached spot price and the cap records the paper `usd`, so true wallet outflow drifts from the cap by slippage + gas + native-price drift, accumulating across a day. Have mirrorLive read native before/after on buys and return the native-in delta; record usd = realizedNativeIn × execution-time native price into recordDailySpend, and use the same measured realized values for P&L. Switch dayKey() to UTC so the reset boundary is deterministic.
3. **Live buys spend full USD in native with no gas reserve — wallet drainable to zero, positions become unsellable** — `server/src/engine.ts:288-304,291,326; server/src/live-solana.ts:51-54`  
   _Fix:_ EVM/Solana buys size the native sell as the full usd; no path reads native balance before buying, and the only clamp is on the sell side for the traded token. Once native hits ~0 there is no gas to approve+sell, so stops/TPs silently fail and a losing position can't be exited. Before each buy, read native balance inside the runExclusive lane and cap the native sellAmount so a configurable reserve (several × avgGasUsd, plus SOL rent) always remains; refuse the buy if already below the reserve.
4. **Unlimited maxUint256 approval to 0x AllowanceHolder + unvalidated quote.transaction.to on the shared wallet** — `server/src/live.ts:90-99,101-107,22-36`  
   _Fix:_ On a sell, the code approves maxUint256 to the 0x-supplied spender and sends calldata to quote.transaction.to verbatim with no allowlist. A compromised/buggy AllowanceHolder or a spoofed/MITM'd 0x response can drain the ENTIRE token balance of the single shared wallet (every bot's holdings on that chain). Approve only the exact sellAmount (or small buffer); validate quote.transaction.to against a hardcoded per-chain set of 0x's published AllowanceHolder/Settler addresses and throw on mismatch; reset stale allowances to 0.
5. **No aggregate/concentration exposure cap across bots sharing one live wallet** — `server/src/engine.ts:672,817-831,934-947,1141; server/src/strategies.ts:117/245/269; server/src/risk.ts positionPct 12/22/35`  
   _Fix:_ Each bot sizes only against its own budget; createBot checks only budget>=10. Nothing caps the sum of bot budgets against real wallet equity or how many bots hold the same token, while auto-sniper (≤10), follows, and tournaments (≤12 at full budget) spawn bots automatically — so 5+ bots can each take a 35% position in the same token, composing into a concentrated bet exceeding actual on-chain funds. Add an account-equity exposure guard and a per-token concentration cap, enforced in createBot and settle's live-buy path.
6. **Confirmed on-chain buy whose post-swap balance read fails spends real funds with zero bookkeeping** — `server/src/engine.ts:279,314-317,342-351,407-410,533-550`  
   _Fix:_ executeSwap returns only after receipt success, so the spend is real; but the post-swap balance read sits inside the try whose catch returns {ok:false}, so on a transient RPC error applyHoldingDelta never runs, recordDailySpend is skipped (cap undercounts), no position opens, and tokens are orphaned. Move recordDailySpend to fire as soon as a hash returns; on a failed after-read, credit realHeld defensively with realHeldUntrusted=true and log the tx hash prominently; retry the after-read before giving up. Never drop a confirmed spend from the ledger or cap.
7. **Crash between buy confirmation and settle's flushNow loses cash/position/spend; bot can re-buy with real funds** — `server/src/engine.ts:406-411,315/343; server/src/store.ts:293-303,322-328`  
   _Fix:_ mirrorLive mutates realHeld in-lane but does NOT flush; the durable write is only at settle line 411 after execBuy. A crash in that window loses cashUsd/position/trade/monthly and undercounts the cap, and on restart the still-'running' bot can buy AGAIN with real native. Call flushNow() inside mirrorLive right after applyHoldingDelta, and write a pending-buy journal + record spend BEFORE the swap so startup can reconcile cash/position/monthly/liveSpend, not just realHeld; pause any bot whose persisted state is inconsistent with on-chain realHeld.
8. **1% DEX slippage cap with no price-impact guard while paper models 0.15%** — `server/src/live.ts:79,86; server/src/live-solana.ts:94,105; server/src/engine.ts:96,127,326,335; server/src/backtest.ts:24`  
   _Fix:_ 0x hardcodes slippageBps '100' and the only liquidity guard is liquidityAvailable===false; Jupiter defaults to 100 and priceImpactPct is never inspected. On the thin pools the sniper targets a small buy is itself a multi-percent impact trade with no abort. Read quote.buyAmount / outAmount + priceImpactPct, compute expected impact vs mid, scale slippageBps down for thin pools, and abort when expected impact exceeds the strategy's modeled edge. Pass explicit slippageBps from the engine instead of letting Jupiter default.
9. **Sniper stop/TP/trailing evaluated only every 20s against a silently-stale DEX price** — `server/src/engine.ts:26,640,468,84-88; server/src/prices.ts:65,74,81-84; server/src/strategies.ts:215-221`  
   _Fix:_ On the most volatile assets the bot trades, exits are checked at most once per 20s on a pool price that falls back to last-known on any fetch failure, with no staleness detection — a token that rugs 50-90% within a tick or stale window is exited far below the configured stop. Timestamp poolCache entries and refuse to act on a stop/TP/trailing decision when the price is stale; treat a stale/missing pool price as a trigger to attempt an immediate exit; poll active sniper pools on a tighter 2-5s interval decoupled from the global tick.
10. **All-Weather adaptive default is marketed as 'Profits in any market' but its crash defense lags a 50-hour SMA** — `server/src/strategies.ts:287,289,293,301,303-316; web/src/App.tsx:113; server/src/risk.ts:25,39,53; server/src/backtest.ts:24`  
   _Fix:_ The default one-click bot's regime exit triggers only at price 6% below a 50-HOUR mean — structurally too slow for a fast crash, and the stop-loss (3/5/8%) fires first while the chop logic bleeds the real 1% live spread on every round trip. The advertised positive edge is a cheap-cost backtest artifact. Remove/soften the 'Profits in any market'/'stays positive in crashing markets' claims (strategies.ts:287,289; App.tsx:113) — describe it honestly as a capital-preserving default, not a money-maker. Add a faster drawdown-from-high/ATR-based crash exit. Re-run profit-finder under realistic costs.
11. **Profit Finder ranks in-sample best-of-N and surfaces survivorship-biased trending/top100 configs** — `server/src/backtest.ts:211-233,276-279,327,355-360,390; server/src/strategies.ts:348; server/src/risk.ts:21,35,49; DOCS.md:79-82`  
   _Fix:_ simulate() fits and reports on the same 30d window with no out-of-sample split or multiple-testing penalty; the headline is the single best of 5×3 configs; top100 backtests already-pumped movers and trending backtests only coins that already trended — and DOCS markets it as the winner to one-click launch live. Add walk-forward and make out-of-sample return the ranked metric, report the median/lower-percentile across the 15 combos, apply realistic costs, and explicitly label top100/trending rows as survivorship-biased and non-predictive.
12. **No graceful shutdown — SIGTERM exits synchronously mid-swap, abandoning a broadcast on-chain buy** — `server/src/store.ts:337-343; scripts/launch.mjs:56; server/src/engine.ts:287/322,314-315,640,50-62`  
   _Fix:_ SIGINT/SIGTERM handlers flushNow then process.exit(0) with no drain; a deploy/reboot/Ctrl-C during a live trade can confirm a buy whose tokens are never recorded (multi-holder tokens are flagged untrusted, never credited), so the bot believes it has cash it already spent. Add a draining shutdown in index.ts: set a draining flag, clearInterval the engine timer, await in-flight lane promises under a bounded race (>tx-confirm time), then flush and exit; remove the synchronous process.exit(0) from store.ts so the app-level handler controls exit.
13. **Single unsupervised engine process — a fatal crash leaves it dead with nothing to restart it** — `server/src/index.ts:387-395; scripts/launch.mjs:51-56; package.json start script`  
   _Fix:_ uncaughtException exits cleanly 'so the process manager can restart' but no process manager exists; launch.mjs is a bare spawn wrapper with no respawn, and start is plain `node scripts/launch.mjs`. While down, stop-losses don't fire and open live positions ride unmanaged. Run under systemd (Restart=always, RestartSec=5, StartLimit* to avoid crash loops) or pm2 with --max-restarts and exponential backoff. Document the exact production launch command (not npm run dev / --watch).
14. **No observability or alerting — the owner can't know a bot halted, a provider died, or the wallet drained** — `server/src/engine.ts:356-359,626,548-549,402; server/src/index.ts:84-103`  
   _Fix:_ There is no /health route and no alerting integration anywhere; haltLive silently pauses a bot with one log line, failures are stdout-only, and there's no dead-man heartbeat. A degraded provider can pause all bots while the owner misses every exit for hours. Add GET /health (last global tick age, mode, #running/#paused, spentToday vs cap, per-provider last-success) and a single env-gated outbound notifier (Telegram/webhook) fired on haltLive, stalled tick loop, daily-cap breach, realHeldUntrusted, and process start/exit. This is the key enabler for safe unattended live operation.
15. **0x sell path awaits maxUint256 approval + swap receipts with no caller timeout, stalling the serialized EVM lane** — `server/src/live.ts:91-98,108; server/src/engine.ts:287,51-62`  
   _Fix:_ A slow-mining approval/swap on a fast sniper exit stalls that token's sell AND every queued op on the evm lane for up to viem's 180s default. Pass an explicit short timeout (30-60s) to both waitForTransactionReceipt calls so a stall fails fast and frees the lane; replace the maxUint256 approval with a bounded amount and read current allowance first to skip re-approval; consider pre-approving tokens out-of-band so the time-critical exit never waits on an approval mine.

## P2 — meaningful, lower urgency

1. **No MEV/sandwich protection — public mempool + 1% slippage is a recurring extraction tax** — Route EVM swaps through a private/protected RPC (flashbots-protect style) instead of plain http(); submit Solana via Jito bundles rather than plain sendRawTransaction; tighten slippageBps below 1% per-pool. Model a realistic sandwich cost in the backtest so paper stops overstating edge. On a small account doing many small swaps into thin pools, this tax routinely exceeds the signal.
2. **CORS is treated as a CSRF backstop but doesn't reject requests server-side** — With ENGINE_TOKEN unset, CORS is the only gate, and the cors middleware always calls next() regardless of origin — so a malicious page's no-cors POST still executes (flips live mode / triggers trades) and any origin-less caller (curl/script) bypasses it entirely. Make ENGINE_TOKEN the enforced fail-closed boundary on mutating routes; if CORS is kept, require a custom header simple requests can't set and reject disallowed/missing origins with next(err). Update the misleading comment at index.ts:44-45.
3. **Unauthenticated WebSocket /ws and GET routes leak live trade activity and burn paid API keys** — /ws broadcasts every bot's positions, P&L, live fills, and copy-traded wallet addresses to any observer; auth-exempt GET routes interpolate unvalidated user input (:address, :cg) into outbound Etherscan/CoinGecko URLs carrying the operator's API key. When ENGINE_TOKEN is set, gate the WS upgrade (timingSafeEqual) and the wallet-address fields in /api/meta; validate :address against /^0x[a-fA-F0-9]{40}$/ and :cg against a safe charset, and encodeURIComponent all user-derived URL segments. Localhost binding remains the primary control.
4. **Control-plane live-money mutations use debounced save(), not flushNow** — createBot/createSniper and the autoScan/followScan poolUsd decrements use the 400ms-debounced (leading-edge) save(), so a just-armed live bot or sniper plus its budget decrement can vanish on SIGKILL/OOM/power-loss after the on-chain swap already moved funds. Call flushNow() for these mutations when mode==='live', mirroring /api/mode and controlBot; optionally make save() trailing-edge so persisted state is at most ~400ms stale.
5. **CoinGecko candle outage freezes indicator bots before SL/TP runs** — tickBot returns on `candles.length < 30` BEFORE decide() runs, but slTp depends only on spot price + position, not candles — so an outage silently disables stop-loss/take-profit on open live positions. Hoist the SL/TP guard out of the candle-gated path: evaluate slTp against the live spot price before the candle early-return, refuse new entries when candle age exceeds a ceiling, and surface a 'pricing degraded' bot state.
6. **20s live ticks re-evaluate hourly-calibrated indicators against a noisy/stale spot the backtest never models** — Live re-runs EMA9/21, RSI14 and the SMA50 regime ~180x/hour on a 20s-cached (or 90s-frozen) spot appended to hourly closes, while the backtest steps one closed hourly bar — so live trade count and cost drag exceed backtest estimates and exits can fire on a single stale print (cooldown bounds re-entry, but first-entries per regime still inflate). Act on closed hourly bars (run decide() only on a new candle.t), or keep the 20s loop only for SL/TP with a confirmation/debounce that skips while backoffRemainingMs()>0.
7. **Fixed-cost drag (under-modeled gas + unmodeled EVM approve tx) makes most small trades uneconomic** — At $36-$66 positions and ~$37 grid slices, the fixed gas plus the first-sell approve tx (modeled nowhere in the backtest) is a dominant, sometimes trade-killing drag the profit-finder never charges. Add the approve gas to the first sell of each token in the backtest, raise avgGasUsd to realistic swap-inclusive figures, and add a minimum-economic-trade guard (e.g. position >= 20× round-trip gas) surfaced in createBot and the ranking.
8. **DCA averages down into a falling token with no trend filter, only a wide -2.5× stop** — DCA gates entries only on the 24h SMA with no 50h downtrend filter and no total-deployment cap, fully deploying its budget before the capitulation stop fires at -12.5%/-20% on average cost — martingale-like averaging into a dying token. Add a long-MA downtrend filter mirroring meanrev/adaptive (skip when >8% below the 50h avg), cap cumulative slices, and tighten the capitulation multiple toward 1.5-2×.
9. **Manual snipe path (POST /api/sniper) enforces no pool-liquidity floor** — createSniper validates token/pool format, budget, TP/SL, and the per-token safety screen, but never reads pool liquidity; the only floor (minLiquidityUsd=2000) lives in the discovery feed, not the write path, so a direct caller can snipe a few-hundred-dollar pool. In createSniper, fetch current liquidityUsd and reject below a floor AND reject when budgetUsd exceeds ~1-2% of liquidity, so the floor applies to every snipe.
10. **Daily-spend cap defaults to OFF in code (0 = unlimited) for fresh installs** — MAX_LIVE_USD_PER_DAY defaults to 0 = no cap in code; this owner's .env sets 50 so the cap is live for THIS deployment, but a fresh/misconfigured install has no cumulative drain bound. Make live mode fail-closed when the cap is <=0, or auto-default it to a fraction of detected wallet balance instead of treating 0 as unlimited.
11. **Live execution uses default public RPCs (no EVM override hook; Solana defaults to mainnet-beta)** — Every live EVM read/approve/send/receipt-poll routes through viem's public default RPC (no override hook exists at all), and Solana defaults to the throttled public mainnet-beta (SOLANA_RPC_URL unset). Public RPCs rate-limit and return stale state under 24/7 load, feeding stale balances into the sell-clamp. Add per-chain EVM RPC env vars passed into http(url), set SOLANA_RPC_URL to a paid endpoint, and fail fast at startup if dedicated RPCs are unset when live is armed.
12. **No fail-fast startup preflight — live misconfiguration surfaces at trade time, not boot** — A revoked Turnkey key, wrong address, or unreachable RPC isn't detected at boot; the failure shows up as a buried 'stayed paper' warn, and a malformed MAX_LIVE_USD_PER_DAY silently parses to 0 (no cap). Add a preflight before server.listen: in live mode assert the matching signer is ready per active chain and do one cheap RPC round-trip; process.exit(1) on failure; reject a NaN cap at boot.
13. **Node launcher silently disables the durable SQLite trade ledger and trusts unverified nvm dir names** — If launch.mjs can't find Node >=22.5 it falls back to ambient Node (project default 18) and recordTrade becomes a no-op, leaving only a 2000-row JSON window for audit/tax — invisible with no /health route. Exit non-zero (or actually run `node -v` on the candidate) when no modern Node is found, read the pinned .nvmrc (24.15.0), and surface tradeDbReady() on /health. Long term run under a fixed Node in the supervisor.
14. **Autopilot spawn loop ignores in-flight Stop/Delete across its await; deleteBot leaks pool budget** — The auto-sniper/follow spawn loops re-check only maxConcurrent/poolUsd across the await, not a.enabled/f.enabled, so 1-N extra live snipes can fire after the user pressed Stop; and deleteBot never reclaims a running bot's perSnipeUsd. Re-read enabled state and re-derive active before each spawn and decrement, abort if disabled, and reclaim budget in deleteBot/deleteFollow. (poolUsd itself does not desync.)
15. **Stat-integrity defects: dust/break-even sells booked as wins; live counter vs SQLite key off different fields; sniper decimals default 18** — Economically-meaningless dust round trips and pnl===0 closes are counted as wins, inflating the exact win-rate the owner would use to judge edge, and the in-memory counter uses raw pnl while the SQLite/monthly aggregates use rounded pnlUsd so they can disagree. Separately, sniper decimals default to 18, so a partial take-profit on a non-18-decimal EVM token dumps the full position. Use strict pnl>0 keyed off one shared field, skip recording dust sells, and fetch real on-chain decimals when arming a sniper.

## Top profit levers (honest, not hype)

- Reconcile live P&L to the actual on-chain fill (engine.ts:406-411 + r.delta) so you can finally tell whether the bot is making or losing money — without this you are flying blind and every other lever is unmeasurable.
- Fix the cost model: charge ~1-1.5%/side (LP fee + impact + the 1% live slippage band) plus the EVM approve-gas leg in backtest.ts/engine.ts, then re-run Profit Finder. Many 'profitable' configs will turn negative — that is the point; you want to fund only configs that survive honest costs.
- Cut execution cost where it actually leaks: tighten slippageBps below 1% per-pool with a price-impact abort, route EVM through a private/MEV-protect RPC and Solana through Jito, and use paid RPCs. On a small account doing many small swaps, fees+MEV are the dominant drag.
- Add walk-forward + multiple-testing penalty to Profit Finder and stop surfacing survivorship-biased trending/top100 rows, so you stop deploying the single most overfit config.
- Stop full-budget sniping: size snipes as a small fixed fraction of equity with a real price-impact and dual-provider screen. The sniper is the only path with asymmetric upside, but as written it is the fastest route to zero — bounded sizing is what keeps it a strategy rather than a lottery ticket.
- Trade fewer, larger, lower-frequency positions on liquid assets. At a few-hundred dollars, every extra round trip is a ~0.3%+ fixed bite plus slippage; reducing turnover is one of the few changes that reliably improves NET return.

## Top risk-of-ruin scenarios

- A single sniper/auto-sniper token rugs or honeypots while holding 100% of the bot's budget (strategies.ts:204), the on-chain exit fails, and the manual-stop path books a phantom win — real tokens stranded, account stepped toward zero.
- An unauthenticated local/LAN caller (no ENGINE_TOKEN, server bound 0.0.0.0) flips live mode and drains the Turnkey Solana wallet via Jupiter — theft, not trading loss.
- A losing live session bleeds the account with no circuit breaker: every 20s tick keeps buying and stopping out at -3/-5/-8% while paying ~1% slippage + gas, and the daily cap resets at midnight to allow full re-spend.
- Stale/frozen price during a 429 backoff or RPC outage: a stop-loss never sees the real drop so a position bleeds uncapped, and/or a live buy is mis-sized from a frozen native price and sends the wrong amount of real ETH/SOL.
- The wallet's native coin is spent to ~0 by full-USD buys (engine.ts:291,326) leaving no gas to approve+sell — losing positions become unsellable.
- A hung 0x/Jupiter fetch with no timeout wedges the serialized lane; stops and the manual-stop liquidation queue behind it forever while an open position rides an adverse move, with no crash and no alert.
- A buggy/compromised 0x AllowanceHolder or spoofed quote.transaction.to drains the entire shared-wallet token balance via the unlimited maxUint256 approval (live.ts:90-107).
